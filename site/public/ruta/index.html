Questions.

Can I leave with my data?

Always. Settings → Download everything gives one file with all your guests, bookings, letterhead and signatures. It loads into a free self-hosted Ruta, or back into the cloud.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. The centre becomes read-only for 60 days and Download everything keeps working. Choose a plan any day to carry on.

Where is our data kept?

Cloud centres run on servers we look after, with an encrypted copy made every night and kept in a second place. On-premise keeps everything on your own computer.

Do we need the internet all day?

Cloud needs a connection to change the day; the printed sheet works without one. On-premise works on the centre's own Wi-Fi.

Is support included in the trial?

The trial is the full app without support. Upgrade on any day of the trial to get support and setup help straight away.

Can we cancel?

Yes, any month. You keep your data: download it, or self-host for free.

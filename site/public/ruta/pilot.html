Pilot notice

Ruta is in a pilot. This is how we handle your centre's data until a lawyer has reviewed a full privacy policy. Last changed 29 September 2026.

Who runs it

Ruta is run by its maintainer, who also answers questions and complaints: WhatsApp +420 777 558 262, helloayursen@gmail.com. We reply within one working day, and act on any request within 30 days.

Whose data it is

Your centre decides what goes into Ruta and is responsible for its residents' data. Ruta keeps and processes it only to run your centre's schedule for you.

What we store and why

Names and phone numbers of staff and residents; stay dates; therapies, schedules, diets, vitals and notes you enter; your letterhead, logo and signatures; the admin's email for sign-in. It is used only to run the schedule, print your sheets and send you sign-in and trial emails. No card details. No ads, never sold or shared, never used to train AI.

Where it is kept

On servers we run for the pilot, behind Cloudflare for HTTPS, with an encrypted nightly copy kept in a second place (Cloudflare R2). Only the maintainer can reach them. A centre on On-premise keeps everything on its own computer.

Getting it out, or deleted

Settings → Download everything gives you all your data in one file at any time, also after a trial ends. Ask us and we delete your centre; trial centres are deleted automatically as the trial emails say. Backup copies roll off within 30 days.

Changes

We announce changes to this notice in the app before they apply.

# UX review of the audit screenshots (22 Oct)

A designer subagent read DESIGN.md and STORIES.md, then the walk and crawl shots, with three lenses: critique, accessibility, copy. Some crawl shots it cites were thinned to four per screen to keep the repo small; inventory.tsv lists them all.

## Findings (most severe first)

I looked at all 111 walk images and about 50 crawl images, at least one from every Menu screen and every distinct sheet type. Seven walk steps came out as "BROKE" (the script timed out), so the screens after those steps were not captured.

1. [high] [UX copy] Card after a shorter stay — once the stay is cut to 18 days, Package still reads "21 days · Rs 98,750" while Accommodation updates to "17 nights · Rs 27,200". Nothing warns the admin that the package no longer fits. — leave-04-card-after-shortening-scroll-0.png — When Stay changes, show "Package is 21 days, stay is now 18 — change package?" on the Package row.
2. [high] [design] Closed-day result — the day screen says "None can move to a later day of their stay", but the need-you sheet for the same treatment offers "Tue 3 Nov … (1 day later, after their stay ends)". The two screens contradict each other, and it offers a date after the guest has left. — holiday-04-day-11-after-closing.png, holiday-05-day-11-what-needs-you-scroll-0.png — Never offer a slot outside the stay; offer only Cancel and say so in the same words on both screens.
3. [high] [UX copy] Settings › Backups — the heading reads "MOVE TO ANOTHER RUTA". "Ruta" means nothing to the admin and looks like a typo. — crawl/settings--backups.png — Rename it to "Move to another computer".
4. [high] [design] Close the centre sheet — it opens on Thu 22 Oct (today) and says "60 treatments are booked then", with the primary button "Close and move them" one tap away. This is the same default-to-today trap as the leave sheet (already known), but here it is on a far bigger action. — holiday-01-close-another-day.png — Leave the date empty and keep the button disabled until a date is picked.
5. [high] [design] Need-you count — the same moment shows three different numbers and names: Menu "8 need you", Patients "Needs attention · 5", Today "DAY · 3". Settings adds "12 of 14 on · the pill shows 8 today". — crawl/menu.png, patients-scroll0.png, 8-need-you-scroll0.png, settings--what-needs-you.png — Use one term ("Needs you") and one count; label sub-counts explicitly ("5 patients, 3 today").

Toasts cover two or three more rows. — breaks-03-marked-didnt-come.png, breaks-06-room-out-of-use.png, rooms-scroll0.png, today-scroll0.png — Add bottom padding the height of the buttons plus the toast, and raise toasts above the buttons.

## Verdict

- The main jobs (admit, book a week, diet changes, leave, close a day, no-show, discharge print) can all be done one-handed on a phone. The design system is applied consistently.
- What blocks trust is contradictions the admin will notice: package against stay, "none can move" against a move offer, and three different need-you counts.
- The words for absence, alerts and settings are inconsistent and partly developer jargon ("pill", "RUTA", "not counted", "{YYYY}"). A non-technical admin will hesitate.
- Floating buttons and toasts that hide list rows, plus low-contrast disabled buttons and colour-only status, are small but appear on every screen.
- Fix items 1–7 (about a week of small PRs) before the pilot; the rest can follow in polish batches.

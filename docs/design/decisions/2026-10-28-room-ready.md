# A room someone left is marked ready when made up (27 Oct)

For #1199 (situation #6). Decided by the session, within the walk-order decision.

- A guest room someone leaves on a day is listed under "To make up" on Guest rooms for that day, with who arrives into it.
- One tap marks it ready ("Ready ✓"); a second tap undoes a mistaken one. The count says how many are ready.
- The room keeps the day it was last made up (`ready_on`). Nothing is blocked: an arrival into a room not yet ready is still allowed, the list is housekeeping's to-do.
- Not built: rooms left on an earlier day and never marked. Add when a centre says rooms stay unmade overnight.

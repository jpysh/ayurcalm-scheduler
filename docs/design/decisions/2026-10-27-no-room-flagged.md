# A guest staying tonight with no room is flagged (27 Oct)

Decided in the unattended stays session for #1256.

- What needs you has a rule, on by default: "Staying tonight with no guest room", one line per guest, opening their card. A day patient is never listed, and a centre with no guest rooms set up sees nothing.
- The guest rooms sheet names them under its counts ("No room given yet, staying tonight (2): …"), so housekeeping knows a bed is still to be found.

# A new stay that has already ended is asked about once (27 Oct)

Decided in the unattended stays session for #1252.

- Adding a stay whose leaving day is before today stops once with the dates and the year ("This stay is in the past: 20 Dec 2025 to 27 Dec 2025. Save anyway?"). "Save anyway" saves it; recording a past stay is allowed.
- The year is said only in this question: the sheet's dates stay short. A stay ending today or later is never asked about.

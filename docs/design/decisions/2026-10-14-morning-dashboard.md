# The maintainer's morning: one message, one page, feedback in it

14 Oct 2026, #818, decided with the maintainer.

Each morning a Telegram message, and the same on a private page, gives sign-ups, who came in and printed their own sheet, trials live, ending and waiting, health, and the week's feedback, with one push line on top picked by a rule (a health problem, then a silent trial about to end, then fresh feedback, then outreach). A hosted centre's "Report a problem or an idea" reaches it directly; a self-hosted one still opens WhatsApp. Each week a session reads the feedback and turns each item into an issue, a polish line or a reply. Later: weekly targets typed by a Telegram reply, and landing visits from Cloudflare Web Analytics.

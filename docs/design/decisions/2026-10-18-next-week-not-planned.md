# "Next week not planned" is a What needs you rule
18 Oct 2026, #805, decided by the maintainer.

A resident whose latest review (a consultation in this stay) is today or past, with no treatment booked in the seven days after it (from today, before the leaving day), raises "Next week not planned". From Patients' Needs attention the row opens Plan next week on that review in one tap; in What needs you a resident with several lines opens their card. On by default, switchable like the other rules. No new screen; planning several residents in one sheet was left out (one wrong tick books the wrong person's week).

# Availability: one place and one form for anything not available

9 Oct 2026, #695. Decided by the maintainer. Mock: `../mocks/availability.html`.
Replaces `2026-10-09-leave-is-people-only.md` (#671).

Leave became people only, which left five different ways to mark something out:
two for staff, two for treatment rooms, one deep in guest rooms, one inside a
therapy's details, one at the bottom of a guest's card. Fresha, Mews and Cliniko
all use one idea for staff and resources, started from the thing itself. So:
"Leave" becomes **Availability** and "Team" becomes **Staff**. One form serves
every kind (From now · Part of a day · Some days, a reason, what it affects), for
one or several things at once. Each thing's sheet has the same rows: Available
again · Not available… · Details. "Save and fix" makes one plan across every day
and thing in the range; the app never fixes anything unasked. Regular absence is
the thing's hours, so the form has no weekly repeat. A guest away is marked on
the kitchen sheet rather than dropped.

Left out: equipment broken while the room still works; a guest's day away before
arrival; a history of past days.

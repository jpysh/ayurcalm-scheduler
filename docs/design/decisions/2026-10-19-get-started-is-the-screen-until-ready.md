# Get started is the whole screen until the centre can book, and stays until the first treatment

19 Oct 2026, #972, decided by the maintainer (stay-until-booked nudge chosen by Claude, to confirm).
A new centre shows no date bar and no day list until therapies, rooms, therapists and a patient exist; the date bar sat mid-screen over an empty day. Then the date bar returns and Get started shrinks to one row, "Book your first treatment", which stays (no dismiss) until any treatment is booked. The example centre shows no checklist; Make it mine is its one call to action. Left out: adding rooms, therapists and therapies in bulk (#973).

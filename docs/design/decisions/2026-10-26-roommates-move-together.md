# Roommates move together
26 Oct 2026, #1191, decided by the maintainer.

Guests who share a room (a couple, a family, a companion) move together. A move, or the room a refusal suggests, applies to everyone in that room on those nights, in one save with one Undo. If the new room has too few beds, the move is refused with a room that fits them all. Extending or shortening a stay is still per guest.

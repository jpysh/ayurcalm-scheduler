# The Book sheet never opens on a dead end
22 Oct 2026, #1052, decided by the maintainer.

- When the chosen day has no free time and the server names the next free one, the sheet moves there and says why in one line ("Today's hours are over. Showing Fri 23 Oct."). The admin can still change the date.
- One green action per sheet: the foot. A refusal's panel keeps its other fixes (add a therapist, change the stay) but not "Try another therapy", which the Therapy line already does.
- The Book button always names the day and time ("Book Vivaan, Thu 15:30"), today included.
- The course note is two lines: "Day 6 of 14", then "Last: Thalam Thu 22 Oct".
- Days in a row reads 1 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 14 / Other; "‹ Someone else" reads "‹ Change patient".

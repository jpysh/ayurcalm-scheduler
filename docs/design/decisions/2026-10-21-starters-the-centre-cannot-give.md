# Starters the centre cannot give stay listed, greyed out with why
21 Oct 2026, #1022, decided by the maintainer.

A starter needing more trained therapists, or a room with equipment, than the centre has stays in the list, greyed out, with every reason that applies ("Elakizhi needs 2 trained therapists · Shirodhara needs a room with shirodhara table"). It cannot be picked, so Book all never includes it. When none can be given, one line under them says what would unlock them. The reasons come from `GET /starter-courses` (`blocked`), never the browser. A refused Book all answers in one line: the team's reason when the team is the cause, else one entry per therapy with its day count, never a day-by-day list.

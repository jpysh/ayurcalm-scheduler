# Arrival notes are off by default; leaving-day items grey until midday

19 Oct 2026, #983, from the ten-day simulation, decided by Claude at the maintainer's request, to confirm.
"Arrival notes not written" was true of nearly every guest every day and put 13 to 47 red rows in What needs you. It is now off by default (a centre that wants it switches it on in What needs you › change the rules) and never names a guest on their leaving day. "Leaves today, no discharge summary" is grey, not counted, before 12:00 in the centre's time, and counts from midday.

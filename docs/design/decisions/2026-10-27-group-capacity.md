# A group's short days are a warning on the group (27 Oct)

Decided in the unattended stays session for #1242.

- The group sheet names the days the group needs more therapist time than is free: each guest (and each held room not yet named) counts as two hours of treatment a day, less what is already booked for them, against the hours the therapists in that day have left after leave and bookings.
- It is a warning with the way out ("Ask a therapist in, or spread the treatments"), never a refusal. Booking each treatment still goes through the guard.
- Two hours is the seeded centre's usual day (two treatments); it is a constant in the server until a centre asks for another.

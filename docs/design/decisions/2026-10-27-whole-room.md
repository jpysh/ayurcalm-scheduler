# A double let as a single is "the whole room" on the stay (27 Oct)

Decided in the unattended stays session for #1253.

- The accommodation sheet has "The whole room for them" when the chosen room has more than one bed. The guest then fills every bed: the room is not offered as free and nobody else can be put in.
- It is refused, plainly, while someone else already sleeps in the room on one of those nights.
- Price is unchanged: the type's price per day is for reference, never an invoice.

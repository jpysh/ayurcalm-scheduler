# Decisions up to 9 Oct

Moved out of DESIGN.md on 18 Oct so that DESIGN.md holds only the rules and the checklist. Later decisions each have their own file in this folder.

- **The stripe is the therapist; red is a row to fix** (7 Oct, #430; decided by Claude, to confirm): the stripe on a day row is its therapist's colour, keyed by a dot of the same colour beside each name in the By-therapist view. A row that must be fixed swaps it for a red stripe on a pale red fill, with the red flag line; no outline, so it is whole on every side and two in a row read as two.
- **The last day is the discharge** (7 Oct, #406; decided by Claude, to confirm): on a patient's leaving day the card opens on the Discharge summary bar, and the doctor's Next line reads "None · leaving today" with no prompt to book.
- **After closing, tomorrow counts** (7 Oct, #458; decided by Claude, to confirm): once today is past the centre's closing time, tomorrow's things to fix join the Menu count and the inbox as a "Tomorrow" section, "Thu 8 Oct · 8 to fix", whose Open moves the day to tomorrow. Only what must be fixed counts; tomorrow's notes do not.
- **After closing, Print gives tomorrow** (7 Oct, #459; decided by Claude, to confirm): once today is past the centre's closing time, the Menu's row reads "Print tomorrow's sheets · Thu 8 Oct" and prints that day; "Change day" still prints any other. The toast names the day it printed.
- **Dates for a government form carry the year** (8 Oct, #494; decided by Claude, to confirm): the Form C sheet shows every date as "12 Mar 1984", not "Mon 12 Mar"; the line still copies as 12/03/1984.
- **A guest arriving later has their stay on the card** (8 Oct, #495; decided by Claude, to confirm): until they arrive the card says "Arrives Fri 9 Oct · leaves Fri 16 Oct", shows the Arrival checklist, and Stay edits the booked stay instead of adding another. Package, Guest room and Form C can be settled before they come.
- **The Log says which part of a stay changed, and what a guest did** (8 Oct, #499; decided by Claude, to confirm): a stay line reads "stay: dates Fri 9 Oct to Thu 15 Oct", "stay: package set" or "Form C marked filed"; what a guest saves on their own link reads "filled in their own details: phone, country" by "the guest", with the field names only, never the values.
- **A guest not here yet opens their link on their first day** (8 Oct, #498; decided by Claude, to confirm): the page says "Your stay starts Sunday 11 October." and shows that day; "Back to today" is still there. Staff links are unchanged.
- **The Patients list shows who arrives soon** (8 Oct, #496; decided by Claude, to confirm): under the guests staying today, an "Arriving soon" group lists guests whose stay starts in the next 7 days (confirmed by the maintainer 8 Oct; first built as 14), nearest first, each "Arrives Fri 9 Oct · leaves Fri 16 Oct". The "In house" count stays the count of guests staying today.
- **A passport photo is kept on the patient** (8 Oct, #510; decided by Claude, to confirm): a "Passport photo" row on the card opens the phone camera; the photo is shrunk on the phone (about 1200 px, 200 kb), kept apart from the patient record, shown small on the Form C sheet (tap to enlarge), and moves with the centre in an export. Only the admin sees it. No text recognition.
- **One question on the leaving day** (8 Oct, #509; decided by Claude, to confirm): from the leaving day and for a week after, the guest's link asks "How was your stay?" (Very good, Fine, Not good, and an optional line), once. The answer is on their card ("Their stay"). Very good and Fine show in grey under Patients in What needs you and are not counted; Not good counts on the pill for a week.
- **The card shows the last readings** (8 Oct, #508; decided by Claude, to confirm): the Doctor section has a "Readings" line with up to three recorded values, newest first ("BP 124/82 · Thu 8 Oct"), nothing when there are none. The arrival box hints at what to write instead of showing an example that looks like a value.
- **Team on another day offers what changes that day** (8 Oct, #518; decided by Claude, to confirm): from today on, a person's row opens "What changes for them on Fri 9 Oct?" (Not in that day, In late, Leaving early, Away for days), each moving what they miss with an Undo toast; their details stay one tap down. A past day still opens the details.
- **Every starter price says it is an example** (8 Oct, #516; decided by Claude, to confirm): a new centre's packages and accommodation types all carry "Example price: change it to yours", including the four of each that are the maintainer's own centre's figures.
- **Maintainer confirmed, 8 Oct:** (a) the passport photo (#510) travels in every export, all data moves with the centre; (b) a guest's "Not good" (#509) is left to the designer: it counts on the pill for a week, Fine and Very good stay grey, nothing nags beyond that; (c) every starter package and room type, including the maintainer's own centre's figures, says "Example price: change it to yours" (#516).
- **A therapist ticks Done** (8 Oct, #559; decided by Claude, to confirm): the therapist's link has a **Done** tick on each treatment, above Room ready. It stores the centre's time; the treatment card's Recorded line reads "Done 10:20". Unticking clears it. Not flagged, not on the pill, not printed: it informs, as the other records do.
- **Plan next week needs no booked review** (8 Oct, #523; decided by Claude, to confirm): a patient with no consultation booked or given still has **Plan next week**; it plans from today, repeating the last seven days. The review a week on is offered only for a patient who has had a consultation to repeat.
- **An SOS counts** (8 Oct, #521; decided by Claude, to confirm): a therapist's SOS from their link is counted on the Menu and the pill and heads the Day section in alert colour with its own Dismiss. Other raised issues stay as grey notes.
- **What needs you says what the Menu says** (8 Oct, #526, #527; decided by Claude, to confirm): Settings' row counts one per patient, as the pill does, and a rule switched off reads "Off · not on the pill" (with what it would have raised), never "Counts on the pill".
- **Search finds a guest by guest room or country** (8 Oct, #525; decided by Claude, to confirm): typing "T2" or "germany" lists the patients who sleep in that guest room (on any stay) or come from that country, in the same Patients group as a name, with the room on the row.
- **The discharge summary offers the card's medicine** (8 Oct, #524; decided by Claude, to confirm): while "Medication during the stay" is empty and the card holds a Medication line, a quiet **Add from their card: …** puts it in as the first medicine. - **The count shows on every screen** (8 Oct, #537; decided by Claude, to confirm): the badge on the hamburger and the Menu's "N need you" row appear on Team, Rooms, Leave and Settings as well as the day and Patients, and open the same sheet.
- **A private link tells time by the centre's clock** (8 Oct, #529; decided by Claude, to confirm): the link's page gets the centre's time from the server, so a guest on a phone set to another timezone is not asked "How was it?" about a treatment that has not happened.
- **Before a purification is a switch, and its warning offers the order** (8 Oct, #528; decided by Claude, to confirm): the therapy sheet has **Before a purification, like Snehapana** beside Once a stay, and the list row says so. - **A printed footer has no empty parts** (8 Oct, #542; decided by Claude, to confirm): the discharge summary's footer reads "Leela Menon · page 1 of 1" before a number is given, and "Leela Menon · DS/2026/0001 · page 1 of 1" after.
- **The review sheet shows this week** (8 Oct, #530; decided by Claude, to confirm): on the doctor's Round, a patient's sheet has, under the last note, **This week**: the last three readings (as the card reads them), the treatments given in the last seven days counted by therapy ("Abhyanga ×3, Shirodhara ×2"), and the diet plan. Read only; the plan box stays the one thing to write.
- **No room has it is named, with a room offered** (8 Oct, #544; decided by Claude, to confirm): when a therapy needs equipment no room has (a consultation's BP monitor and examination bed), the booking sheet says "No room has a bp monitor and an examination bed, so Consultation cannot be booked yet" and offers **Add a room that has them**, which opens the Rooms sheet with those ticked, instead of "Nothing is free for the rest of their stay".
- **After closing, Book opens on tomorrow** (8 Oct, #546; decided by Claude, to confirm): once today is past the centre's closing time, **+** on the day screen opens the booking sheet on tomorrow, as Print and the inbox already look to tomorrow. - **The Log is not written by looking** (8 Oct, #550; decided by Claude, to confirm): opening a Settings sheet marks it reviewed once; opening it again writes nothing.
- **A guest's details are asked for while they are here or coming** (8 Oct, #549; decided by Claude, to confirm): the link's "Your details" row is left out on the leaving day and after; it says "before you arrive" only to a guest who has not arrived, and "Please add the rest" to one who is here.
- **Large text cuts nothing** (8 Oct, #556; decided by Claude, to confirm): at 200% text a row's title may take three lines, its facts and a picker's note wrap in full, and a page's title wraps beside its count; only a name in a long list is ever cut with an ellipsis.
- **A booking shows itself** (8 Oct, #553; decided by Claude, to confirm): when the booking lands on another day than the one on screen, closing the sheet moves the screen to that day, and the note with Undo names it ("Booked Meera: Shirodhara at 10:45, Sat 10 Oct").
- **Guest rooms are added and changed on their own screen** (8 Oct, #559; decided by Claude, to confirm): the Guest rooms screen has the + like every screen that adds ("Add guest rooms"), and a "Guest rooms · Names and beds" line under its lists opens every room by type, a tap to change one; Team's Therapies and Events lines are the precedent. The screen's rows keep their daily job (a guest's card, or New patient), which is why a room is not changed by tapping its row. One component serves the + , the line and Settings → Packages and accommodation → a type, where the type's sheet has one "Guest rooms" line instead of its own copy. "T1–T6" still adds six; a room with past stays is retired; with no accommodation type the add sheet offers to add one. The type of an existing room is not changed (decided by Claude: its stays carry the type, so a move would need its own consequence line).
- **The guest rooms sheet** (8 Oct, #559; decided by Claude, to confirm): the print toast's fifth sheet, shown only when the centre has guest rooms, kept under Settings → Printed sheets as "Guest rooms". One A4: counts, "Make up first, someone arrives into: T4", then each type's rooms in order with a tick box on the rooms that need doing, "Make up · Sneha leaves today", "Ready for Ishita · until Wed 28 Oct", who is staying on and until when, or Free. Hotel housekeeping sheets order work as turnovers, departures, stayovers, arrivals; here the first of those is lifted to the top and the rest stay in room order, since a small centre walks its rooms by house. No cleaned or dirty state yet.

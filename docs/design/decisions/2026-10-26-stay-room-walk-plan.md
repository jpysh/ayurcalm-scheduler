# Stay and room work: order and how it is checked
26 Oct 2026, #1186–#1197, decided by the maintainer.

The day is simulated at Babaji's size (about 25 rooms, 40 beds) and at a mid-size centre, on a throwaway stack. The maintainer's order is: never double-book, correct sheets, a clear room picture, then fewest taps. Every finding is an issue fixed over time, one small PR each. The work runs in this order: bugs, then room out of use, retire and roommates, late arrival and pause, companions, then groups. Cosmetics go in one polish-batch PR on the stay screens. A final walk runs on the refreshed demo before launch. One admin, up to two phones; 4 to 6 simultaneous saves of the last bed did not double-book it (0 of 15 tries).

# Get started keeps an OPD room apart from the treatment rooms
25 Oct 2026, #1147, decided by the maintainer. Replaces the 24 Oct decision (#1144).

A doctor sees residents in an office or OPD room, never a treatment room. Get started asks for the consultation room on its own, named "OPD" by default and renamable, and lists only treatment rooms under "Treatment rooms". The example's first consultation room becomes the OPD room (or one is made when the example has none); its other consultation rooms go out of use. Treatment rooms keep their equipment unchanged.

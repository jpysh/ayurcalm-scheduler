# A late arrival is not counted for the meals before it; dinner is kept (27 Oct)

Decided by the maintainer on 27 Oct, on #1255.

- A stay can carry an arrival time ("Arriving late…" on the stay sheet). On that day the kitchen sheet leaves the guest out of the meals served before it, by the centre's meal window when it has one, and names them under "Arriving late — not counted for".
- Dinner is always kept: it can be prepared and kept for them.
- This narrows #695 for arrivals only. A guest marked away still keeps every meal.

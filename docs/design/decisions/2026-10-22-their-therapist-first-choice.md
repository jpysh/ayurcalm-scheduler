# A resident's own therapist is the first choice, never the only one
22 Oct 2026, #1043, decided by the maintainer.

The switch "Only their own therapist" is gone. A resident has one setting: their therapist, or none.

- Booking: the sheet opens on their therapist's next free time. If someone else is free sooner, one line offers that ("Sooner with Priya · today 09:30"). The admin can pick any therapist and see that one's times.
- Courses: each day with their therapist when free, else the best qualified one free. The course says how many days each therapist has.
- Their therapist on leave: the plan keeps the time with another qualified therapist, selected, and offers a later day with their own.
- Gender match is unchanged: a hard rule when the therapy and Settings ask for it.

# A late arrival or a pause asks: cancel the missed days or push the stay later
26 Oct 2026, #1193 and #1194, decided by the maintainer.

When a guest arrives days late, or pauses treatments (illness, menstruation in Panchakarma), the app asks. Either cancel the treatments on the missed days and keep the leaving day, or push the stay and its treatments later by the same number of days, re-planned in one pass. Some guests have a fixed flight and some a fixed number of days, so only the admin knows. Each choice has a preview and one Undo.

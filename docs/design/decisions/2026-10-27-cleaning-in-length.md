# Cleaning time is part of a therapy's length, not a room setting (27 Oct)

Decided by Claude for #1303; confirmed by the maintainer on 27 Oct: "cleaning times are considered within therapy slots so no need for special arrangement".

- An oil room needs a few minutes between guests. That time goes in the therapy's length: a 60-minute Abhyanga with 15 minutes of cleaning is set as 75 minutes.
- No turnover minutes per room or in Settings. A second hidden number (`buffer_minutes`) was removed before because nothing refused a booking inside it and no screen or sheet showed it (server/CLAUDE.md).
- The Therapies sheet now says so on the length: "How long, with cleaning the room".
- If a centre needs different cleaning per room for the same therapy, open a new issue with the case.

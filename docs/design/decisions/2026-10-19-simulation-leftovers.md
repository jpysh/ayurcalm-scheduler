# What was left out of the simulation fixes, and why
19 Oct 2026, decided by the maintainer after #979–#987.

- **Not built:** naming the bottleneck skill on the week line (#979). The plan already names it when a day is being fixed (#987), which is when the admin can act.
- **Not built:** a review list on the printed doctor sheet (#981), and "add a visiting therapist" on the plan (#987).
- **Not built:** a purification starter course (#984). Purification follows the doctor's dates, and #986 fills its diets from them; a starter spreads sessions evenly and would break the order.
- **Built:** "Full from …" on the booking sheet (#1014).
- **Starters are edited on Plan next week only**, with Update when one is in use (#1016); no screen under Therapies until a centre asks.
- **A new centre gets starters built from its own therapies** (#1015): admins add the therapies they offer, not the ailments they treat, so a default keeps only therapies the centre has.

# Missing guest papers are one rule in What needs you (27 Oct)

Decided in the unattended stays session for #1251.

- One rule, "Papers", on by default, one line per guest here, naming the first thing missing: no ID number; a foreign guest's visa ending before they leave; a stay whose leaving day changed after its Form C was filed ("tell the FRRO").
- Filing Form C now remembers the leaving day it was filed with, so an extension afterwards is noticed.
- Nothing refuses a booking or a stay over papers: a guest often brings their passport on arrival.
- The seed gives nearly every guest an ID number; one in twenty has none, so the rule shows on the demo.

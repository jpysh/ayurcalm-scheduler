# A guest moved mid-stay changes room from a date, not the whole stay
26 Oct 2026, #1164, decided by Claude in an unattended session.

A stay keeps one current guest room. A move while the stay is under way takes effect from the centre's today (or a date the request names); the nights before it are kept as earlier rooms (`EarlierGuestRoom`: the room, and the date it was left). Only the nights from the move are checked against the new room, so a room someone left yesterday no longer blocks it. Moving back on the same day, as Undo does, leaves no earlier room. A stay not yet begun still changes room for every night.

Not a split stay: a stay carries arrival, discharge, Form C, diet and feedback, and two stays would read as a departure and an arrival.

On the day of the move the guest rooms sheet makes up the old room and readies the new one; the day sheet prints the room slept in on that date.

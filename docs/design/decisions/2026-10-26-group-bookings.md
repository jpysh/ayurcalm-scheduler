# Groups: a group name first, then held rooms
26 Oct 2026, #1196, decided by the maintainer.

Babaji gets 1–2 groups a month of 10–30 guests. Step 1 is a group name on stays, so the admin can find the group, see it on the sheets and move its dates in one action, with anyone not moved listed. Step 2 holds a block of rooms per type before the names arrive. A group has a shared programme with individual treatments on top. Groups come after the gaps in what already exists.

# The centre's opening hours are a rule, like a therapist's (27 Oct)

Decided by Claude for #1297; confirmed by the maintainer on 27 Oct: a booking outside the hours is not allowed, "since staff will not be available or center not under operations".

- A treatment that starts before opening or ends after closing is refused when booked, and one already there is flagged by the day check and in What needs you ("Outside the centre's hours"), with a move planned with the rest of the day in one pass.
- This is what makes shortened hours visible: closing changed from 20:00 to 17:00 flags every treatment that now runs past 17:00.
- No planner tier offers "same time" for such a treatment: it is still after closing.
- Before Settings exist (a fresh install mid-setup) nothing is refused.
- Saving Settings with shorter hours says how many booked treatments now fall outside them, with Fix them opening the day check on the first such day; the day check is where they are moved.

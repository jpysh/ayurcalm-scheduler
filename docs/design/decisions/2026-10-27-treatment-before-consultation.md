# A treatment before the first consultation is a note, never a refusal (27 Oct)

Decided by the maintainer on 27 Oct, on #1241.

- Booking a treatment before the stay's first consultation always saves, with no "Book anyway" step. Routine treatments often need no consultation, and a guest may ask for one while the doctor is away.
- The booking sheet says so before booking, the booking's reply says so, and the day check lists it under "Treated before their first consultation" as a note (worth knowing), never a problem to fix.

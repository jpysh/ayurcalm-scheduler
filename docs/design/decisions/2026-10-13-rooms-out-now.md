# The Rooms count means "out now", not "out some time today"

13 Oct 2026, #772, decided by Claude, to confirm.

The "N out" on the Rooms header and the menu hint counts a room only while its outage is running on the centre's clock. A room out from 14:00 is not counted at 06:30, the same way a part of today that has ended is no longer out (#743). The room's own row still says when it goes out, so nothing is hidden. Left out: a separate "out later" count.

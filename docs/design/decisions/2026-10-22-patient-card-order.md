# The patient card's order (22 Oct, #1077)

The maintainer found the card of a guest arriving later hard to read and asked for the whole card to be reviewed. Decided:

- **Top:** who and when ("Arrives tomorrow, Fri 23 Oct"), then only what still needs doing. A finished checklist is one quiet line ("Arrival ✓ All 4 ready"); an unfinished one names what is left. The discharge bar sits at the top only in the last three days; before that it is a line under Records.
- **Then the days:** today's treatments, then Next days ("First days" before arrival, the arrival day marked "Arrives").
- **Plan next week stays before the review** (maintainer: the admin may plan without the doctor), as a quiet button, not the green main action.
- **Doctor:** before arrival, no "Last seen", and no "Next" when that consultation is already in the days.
- **Stay** (dates, package, accommodation, diet, preferred therapist), then **Arrival notes** in the first days, then **Records** (discharge summary, follow-up, Form C, details, passport photo, private link).
- "Their therapist" reads **Preferred therapist**, "No preference" when unset. A package shorter or longer than the stay says so on its line.
- Before arrival the private link reads "Send for their details" (#489).

# The first centre runs on the Mac until Oracle is ready; ten trials at most

14 Oct 2026, #816, decided with the maintainer.

The first real centre runs in Docker on the Mac mini, through the existing Cloudflare tunnel at its own jains.es address, with each night's backups copied to R2. Docker Desktop gets 8 GB: a centre measured about 150 MB idle and 300 MB printing, so 8 GB holds 35 to 40, but the sign-up stops at 10 trials so a busy morning cannot slow the real centre. Outages are covered by the printed morning sheet and the alerts (#248); a UPS only after a real outage. The move to Oracle happens as soon as it is ready, on a quiet evening, after one timed rehearsal.

# Guest room tags are shown, not filtered (27 Oct)

Decided in the unattended stays session for #1201.

- A guest room can carry short tags ("ground floor", "quiet", "step-free", or the centre's own), set on the room's sheet under "For guests who need".
- They are shown wherever a room is chosen and on Guest rooms. There is no filter: a centre has a few dozen rooms at most, and the admin picks from a short list.

# (Replaced by 2026-10-09-availability.md, #695) Leave holds people only

9 Oct 2026, #671 (server part #670). Decided by the maintainer.

The Leave sheet's "Who or what" list mixed staff, a closed centre, treatment rooms,
guest rooms, therapies and patients under a sheet titled "New leave". Rooms and
therapies do not go on leave, and each job starts where the problem is: a broken
room at the room, a guest's day trip at the guest. So Leave lists therapists and
doctors only. A treatment room or guest room is marked out of use from its edit
sheet, a therapy "not given" from its edit sheet, a patient's "no treatments on
these days" from their card. "Closed for a day" leaves the picker; the closed-days
row already does it. This replaces the 8 Oct #563 rule that a guest room out of
use is Leave's.

Therapy and patient off-days were read only by the auto-booker. They are now
refused by the guard, raised by the day check and avoided by the planner (#670).

# The stay's dates come first on the guest's card (28 Oct)

Decided 28 Oct, amending `2026-10-22-patient-card-order.md`.

- The Dates line (or "Stay" / "New stay" for a guest with none) moves from the Stay group to the top of the card, under who and when and the checklist, above Treatments today.
- Why: changing a stay is the card's most common job, and below the week it took a scroll (3 taps plus 1 scroll); now it is 3 taps.
- The rest of the 22 Oct order is unchanged: the Stay group keeps package, accommodation, diet and preferred therapist. Printed sheets are not affected.

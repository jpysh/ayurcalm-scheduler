# A man and a woman from different bookings in one room: a warning (27 Oct)

Decided in the unattended stays session for #1202.

- When a guest is given a room where a guest of the other gender sleeps on a shared night, and they are not in the same group, the room is still given and a warning says who they share with. A couple who booked separately is the usual reason, so it is never a refusal.
- Same group counts as the same booking. A guest whose gender is "other" raises nothing.
- Checked by the server on adding a patient and on changing a stay's room; the screen only shows the warning.

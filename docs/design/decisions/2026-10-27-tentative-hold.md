# A tentative hold is a held block with a last day (27 Oct)

Decided in the unattended stays session for #1203.

- A phone enquiry's room is held the same way as a group's: Patients › Groups › "Hold rooms for a group or enquiry", with "Tentative" on and a "Keep until" day (three days ahead by default, no later than arrival).
- Until that day the rooms show as taken to everyone else. After it they are free again on their own, and the enquiry's line on Groups says "Lapsed". Confirming is adding the guest, which fills the hold, or switching Tentative off.

# A guest's private link stops 30 days after their stay
18 Oct 2026, #827, decided by the maintainer.

A patient's link opens nothing once their last stay ended more than 30 days ago; it reads "This link is no longer valid. Ask the centre for a new one." A new stay for a returning guest drops the old token, so Send link gives them a fresh one and the link forwarded last time stays dead. A patient with no stay yet keeps theirs. No setting. Staff links are unchanged (switching access off stops them).

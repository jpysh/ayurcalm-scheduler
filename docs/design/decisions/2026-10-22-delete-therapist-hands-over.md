# Deleting a therapist hands their treatments on (#1080)

Decided by the maintainer, 22 Oct 2026.

Deleting a therapist no longer deletes their coming treatments. The sheet's
button reads "Hand their N treatments to others first": the therapist comes off
the team, then each day with their treatments is planned once by the same planner
leave uses, with the resident's own therapist first. A question leave would ask
(a later day, or this time only) takes the planner's first answer, because the
therapist is not coming back. What nobody can take is left without a therapist,
listed once on screen, and waits under What needs you. Done treatments stay on
the record.

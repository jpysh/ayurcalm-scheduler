# Record a therapist's leave

**What it is for:** the leave is recorded, and the day is replanned if you want.

**How:**
1. ☰ Menu → **Availability** → **Not available…**
2. Choose the person, then the days. Add a reason if you like.
3. The app says how many treatments this affects, and shows **The plan** to cover them.
4. **Accept the plan**, or **Save, fix later**.

**Good to know**
- Leave already recorded is listed under *Coming up*. Tap it to change or delete it.
- A whole-centre closure goes under **Centre closed days**.
- After leave is saved, nobody can book that person on those days.

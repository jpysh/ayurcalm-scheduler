# Review a patient's week and plan the next

**What it is for:** after the review, next week is booked and no day is empty by accident.

**How:**
1. ☰ Menu → **Patients**, then tap the patient.
2. The card shows the coming days and the doctor's note.
3. Tap **Plan next week**. The app suggests a treatment for each day of the stay.
4. Check it and tap **Book all**.

**Good to know**
- A day with nothing booked shows as empty on the card, and in **Things to fix or decide**.
- Days the patient should rest, or therapies the doctor ruled out, are left out of the plan.

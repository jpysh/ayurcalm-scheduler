# Add an arriving patient

**What it is for:** the patient is in, with a consultation booked.

**How:**
1. ☰ Menu → **Patients**, then ＋ (**New patient**).
2. Type the name, choose woman or man, and the arrival and leaving dates.
3. Save. The first consultation with the doctor is put on the day for you.

**Many at once:** in the setup (*Make it mine*) or on Patients, paste a list copied from WhatsApp or Excel, one person per line. The app shows each row before saving; fix the rows in red first.

**When the app says no:** it names the field: no name, woman or man not chosen, or leaving before arriving.

# Form C for a foreign guest

**What it is for:** every foreign guest is reported to the FRRO within 24 hours of arrival.

**How:**
1. Open the guest's card. A foreign guest shows a **Form C** line with the date it is due.
2. Tap it. Each line is one field of the FRRO form, in the same order.
3. Tap a line to copy it, and paste it into indianfrro.gov.in.
4. When it is sent, tap **Mark Form C filed**.

**Good to know**
- Missing fields (passport, visa) are counted on the line, so you know before you start.
- A Form C that is due or overdue shows in **Things to fix or decide**.

# Book or move a treatment

**What it is for:** the patient has a treatment at a free time, and you chose it.

**Book:**
1. Tap ＋ in the bar.
2. The app offers the next free times and the patients most behind on their stay. Tap one.
3. Check the therapy, therapist and room, and tap **Book**.
4. Someone not listed? Type their name in the search at the bottom.

**Move:**
1. Tap the treatment on the day.
2. Change the time, therapist or room from the lists. Only people and rooms that are free are offered.

**When the app says no:** it says why in one line: the therapist is busy or away, the room is taken, the patient already has a treatment then, or the therapy is ruled out for this patient. Pick another time from the suggestions.

# The doctor's consultation and the daily round

**What it is for:** every patient sees the doctor on their first day, and the round covers everyone.

**How:**
1. A new patient gets a consultation on arrival day automatically.
2. ☰ Menu → **The daily round** lists every patient the doctor should see today.
3. 🖨 → **Doctor sheet** prints the round.
4. The doctor's notes go on the patient's card, under the doctor section.

**Good to know**
- The doctor can have their own private link (see *Share the day with staff*) to see the round on their phone.
- A patient with no consultation yet shows in **Things to fix or decide**.

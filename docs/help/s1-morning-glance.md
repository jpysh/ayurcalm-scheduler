# Is today OK, and what needs me?

**What it is for:** knowing in a few seconds whether you have to do anything today.

**How:**
1. Open the app. It opens on today.
2. Look at the ☰ Menu button. A number on it means that many things need you. No number: the day is fine.
3. Tap ☰ Menu → **Things to fix or decide**. Each problem is one line, with its fix as the button.
4. Tap the button to accept the fix, or **Open ›** to look first.

**Good to know**
- *For your action* needs a decision. *For your information* is just to know.
- **Nothing else needs you.** means you are done.
- Every fix can be undone from the treatment's history.

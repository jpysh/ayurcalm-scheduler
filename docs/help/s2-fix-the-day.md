# Fix the day when a therapist or room drops out

**What it is for:** everyone still gets their treatment when a therapist calls in sick or a room cannot be used, and you can put it back.

**How (a therapist):**
1. ☰ Menu → **Availability** → **Not available…**
2. Choose the therapist and the days. The app shows how many treatments are affected.
3. Look at **The plan**: who takes each treatment, and when.
4. Tap **Accept the plan**. Or **Save, fix later** to record it now and decide later.

**A room:** the same, choosing the room. The plan moves the treatments to another room.

**Good to know**
- The plan is made for the whole day at once, so two treatments never land in the same room at the same time.
- Undo puts the day back exactly as it was.
- If the app cannot find cover, it says which treatments are left, so you can call someone in or move the patient.

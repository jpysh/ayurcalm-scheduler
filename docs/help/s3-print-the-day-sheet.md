# Print or share the day sheet

**What it is for:** the notice board shows the right day.

**How:**
1. Tap 🖨 in the bar at the bottom.
2. Choose **Print the day's sheets** (or **Print tomorrow's sheets** in the evening).
3. Pick the sheet: **Patient sheet**, **Therapist sheet**, **Doctor sheet**, **Kitchen sheet** or **Guest rooms sheet**.
4. The PDF opens. Print it, or share it on WhatsApp from your phone.

**Good to know**
- If something on the day still needs fixing, the app says so with **Fix first ›**. You can still print.
- ☰ Menu → **Settings** → *Printed sheets* keeps every day's copy for 90 days.
- Changed something after printing? Print again; the sheet always shows the day as it is now.

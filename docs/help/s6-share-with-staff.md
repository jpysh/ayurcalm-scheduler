# Share the day with staff by link and WhatsApp

**What it is for:** each therapist knows their day without calling the office.

**How:**
1. ☰ Menu → **Staff**, then tap the person.
2. Tap **Share their link**.
3. Tap **Send on WhatsApp**, or copy the link and send it any way you like.

**Good to know**
- The link is private to that person. It always shows the current day, so you send it once, not every morning.
- They do not need an account or a password.
